    env: gp.Env
):
    """

            A 1D array of shape (N,) where item_weights[i] represents the weight of item i.

            The constant maximum weight capacity (W) of each bin.

            A Gurobi environment object to use for model creation.
            
    Note:

    Returns:
        gp.Model
